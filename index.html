Our Story

One cart. One recipe. One promise.

The story of how a family recipe from Amritsar became Ahmedabad's favourite gali.

Our Founder

It began in a Punjab kitchen

Our founder grew up watching their grandmother knead dough before sunrise, the whole street waking to the smell of fresh kulchas. That recipe — never written down, only felt — travelled with the family to Ahmedabad.

What started as a single cart is now a place where families gather, students linger, and first dates turn into anniversaries. The recipe hasn't changed. It never will.

Our Mission

To serve authentic Punjabi street food so honest and delicious that no one leaves our gali hungry — or a stranger.

Our Vision

To bring the warmth of a Punjab gali to every city in India, one handmade kulcha at a time.

Our Values

Authenticity over trends. Fresh over fast. Family over transactions. Every single day.
